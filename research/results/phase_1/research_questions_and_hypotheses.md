# Research Questions and Hypotheses

Machine-readable definitions are in `research_questions.json` and
`hypotheses.json`. RQ1-RQ3 are primary; RQ4-RQ6 support interpretation.

## Research questions

| ID | Focus | Primary outcome | Conditions for an answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | Full-framework relevance against area-first and simpler methods | NDCG@10 | Frozen common universe, routes, independent labels, and all baseline outputs |
| RQ2 | Multi-destination routes, importance, and commute limits | NDCG@10 | Valid frozen routes and enough one-, two-, and three-destination profiles |
| RQ3 | User decision quality under destination-first versus area-first discovery | Selected-property relevance | Ethics approval, counterbalanced study, and blind outcome labels |
| RQ4 | KNN, WSM, Rent Fairness, amenities, and preferred-size contribution | NDCG@10 | Controlled candidates/ablations and active-component profiles |
| RQ5 | Stability across requirements, geography, routes, and pools | Rank-biased overlap at 10 | Prespecified perturbations and sufficient subgroup coverage |
| RQ6 | XGBoost error and interpretation across segments | Official-test MAE and subgroup MAE | Frozen predictions and prespecified segments; no test-set retuning |

Each JSON question also records independent/dependent variables, datasets,
comparisons, supporting metrics, analyses, confounders, and limits.

## Hypotheses

| ID | RQ | Comparison | Null and alternative | Unit and planned test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | RQ1 | Full versus area-first, filtering, KNN-only, WSM-only | H0: no positive paired NDCG@10 difference; H1: positive difference | Profile; paired permutation, bootstrap CI, Holm adjustment |
| H2 | RQ2 | Full versus no-destination, equal-importance, no-constraint, and no-location variants | H0: destination components do not improve NDCG@10; H1: at least one declared destination-aware contrast improves it | Route-valid profile; paired tests and destination-count interactions |
| H3 | RQ3 | Destination-first versus area-first workflow | H0: equal selected-property relevance after participant/task effects; H1: destination-first improves it | Participant-task; mixed-effects ordinal model |
| H4A | RQ4 | Full versus no KNN | H0: KNN does not change NDCG@10; H1: it changes quality and/or cost | Profile; two-sided paired test with separate efficiency reporting |
| H4B | RQ4 | Full WSM reranking versus KNN ordering | H0: WSM does not improve NDCG@10; H1: it improves it | Profile on identical shortlist; paired test |
| H4C | RQ4 | Full versus no Rent Fairness | H0: no NDCG@10 change; H1: criterion inclusion improves it | Profile with complete stored assessments; paired test |
| H5 | RQ5 | Base rankings versus prespecified perturbations | H0: overlap is no greater than chance reference; H1: overlap exceeds chance with quantified quality/coverage | Profile-perturbation pair; bootstrap versus seeded random reference |
| H6 | RQ6 | Rent, size, geographic, and fallback groups | H0: equal expected absolute error; H1: at least one group differs | Official-test record; bootstrap contrasts and omnibus permutation where feasible |

No effect size is assumed in advance. A failure to reject any null is
inconclusive unless a separate equivalence design and practically meaningful
margin were justified before seeing results.

## Evidence logic

- Recommendation hypotheses require independent 0-3 labels, not suitability
  scores generated by DhakaNest.
- H1 and H2 require end-to-end results plus coverage and empty-result rates;
  ranking quality alone can hide methods that return very few properties.
- H4 comparisons use controlled candidate sets or precisely declared candidate
  changes so candidate selection is not confused with reranking.
- H6 evaluates predictive reliability, not causal market fairness or exact
  market value.
- Historical NDCG `0.9822` and `0.9615` remain preliminary older-version claims
  and are not evidence for any Phase 1 hypothesis.
